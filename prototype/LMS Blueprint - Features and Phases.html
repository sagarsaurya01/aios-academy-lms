Classes
Recorded + live
Live on Zoom or Google Meet, in batches.

Three things no platform does well. These are our edge.

  1. Company seats. A business owner buys 10 seats, gives them to staff, and sees each person's progress. Only Edmingle comes close, and only on its top plan.
  2. Automatic GST invoices to every buyer. None of the Indian platforms clearly offers this. Our MSME buyers need it to claim input credit.
  3. AI marking of assignments, with a trainer approving the result. None of the 10 platforms offers it.

Why build instead of renting

Renting (e.g. Graphy)

₹25,000 to ₹1,00,000 a year plus 5–10% of every sale. No company seats, no GST invoices, and the student data sits on their platform.

Our own on Frappe

No cut on sales. We own the students and the data. A small server is enough. The real cost is a developer's time for the "To build" rows.

Decisions still open

  • Name of the platform.
  • Hosting: our own server, or Frappe Cloud (managed, monthly fee).
  • Who builds it: an in-house developer, a Frappe partner firm, or Claude with one person to deploy.
  • Pricing model: pay per course, monthly membership, or both.
  • Investment system: this is a separate project. The REA tool doesn't help with either one. We already have the family portfolio tracker for investments.
